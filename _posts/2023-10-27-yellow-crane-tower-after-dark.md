The approach to Yellow Crane Tower was almost as memorable as the tower itself. At the base, cooler shades of purple and green lit the paths and water. They looked lovely against the vivid red lanterns and the bright reds waiting at the tower.

![Purple lights reflected in the water near Yellow Crane Tower]({{ '/images/yct1-2023-10-27.jpeg' | relative_url }})

![Green lights along the path and red lanterns near Yellow Crane Tower]({{ '/images/yct2-2023-10-27.jpeg' | relative_url }})

From farther away, the tower had seemed smaller. Standing directly underneath it changed that impression completely. I looked up at its tiers, each one edged in light, with the red glow filling the spaces between them.

![Looking up at the red and gold illuminated Yellow Crane Tower]({{ '/images/yellow-crane-tower2-2023-10-27.jpeg' | relative_url }})

The evening ended with a light show. The tower glowed red and gold while green beams cut through the dark around it. A fitting finale.

![Yellow Crane Tower lit red and gold during the light show, framed by green beams]({{ '/images/yct4-2023-10-27.jpeg' | relative_url }})
