I landed in Shenzhen at an airport that felt enormous by my Ottawa standards. It was my first impression of the city's scale.

{% include responsive-image.html src="/images/shenzhen_airport-2023-10-22.jpeg" alt="Shenzhen airport on arrival" article=true %}

From the hotel, I could see the harbour. In the mornings I would wait at the window for the first beams of sunlight to break over the horizon.

{% include responsive-image.html src="/images/Shenzhen-hotel-view-231025.jpeg" alt="Harbour view from the hotel in Shenzhen" article=true %}

I could also take a morning walk along the dock near the hotel. The ocean seemed to go on forever. It was a lovely way to start the day, looking out over the water.

{% include responsive-image.html src="/images/hotel_morning_walk-2023-10-23.jpeg" alt="Morning view across the water from the dock near the Shenzhen hotel" article=true %}

I went up Kingkey 101, and the elevator was strikingly fast. At the top, I could look over Shenzhen's cityscape and see parts of Hong Kong in the distance. From up there, the buildings, roads, and hills seemed to stretch in every direction.

{% include responsive-image.html src="/images/kingkey101-2023-10-23.jpeg" alt="View over Shenzhen from Kingkey 101" article=true %}

Later, I wandered through the tech markets. There seemed to be an endless supply of electronics, and I loved getting lost among them just to see what I could find.

{% include responsive-image.html src="/images/huaqiang_tech_plaza-2023-10-23.jpeg" alt="Huaqiang tech plaza with electronics shops across several floors" article=true %}

{% include responsive-image.html src="/images/Shenzhen-tech-market-231024.jpeg" alt="Electronics at a Shenzhen tech market" article=true %}

I also visited a fancy mall with a skating rink inside it. An ice rink in the middle of a shopping trip was quite a sight.

{% include responsive-image.html src="/images/skating_rink_in_mall-2023-10-24.jpeg" alt="Skating rink inside a Shenzhen mall" article=true %}

I ended an evening walking around the hotel. The paths were well developed, and the atmosphere made it easy to keep walking a little longer.

{% include responsive-image.html src="/images/Shenzhen-evening-walk-231023.jpeg" alt="Evening walk in Shenzhen" article=true %}
