I have never sweat the way I did in Japan's humidity. My thin travel pants were completely soaked and sticking to my legs. My arms were so wet they might as well have just come out of the shower.

{% include responsive-image.html src="/images/Japan-humidity-20240831.jpeg" alt="My hand and wrist slick with sweat in Japan's humidity" article=true %}

I knew it would be humid. I did not know it would be *that* humid. Unreal.
