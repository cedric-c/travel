Tokyo Tower was especially beautiful in the evening. At the top, I looked out at red lights blinking across the city. They seemed to go on forever, an endless sea of red in the dark. I stayed with that view for a while.

![Tokyo at night from inside the tower, with red lights blinking across the skyline]({{ '/images/Tokyo-tower-20231108.jpeg' | relative_url }})

Back at the bottom, the tower cast a bright orange glow over everything around it. The colour showed up in every reflection. Looking up from there felt just as good as looking out from above.

![Tokyo Tower glowing orange against the night sky]({{ '/images/Tokyo-tower-outside-20231108.jpeg' | relative_url }})

The definition of atmosphere.
