October is almost here. I'm heading from Ottawa to Tokyo, Hong Kong, and Chongqing, and I wanted a place for the things that don't fit neatly into an itinerary.

I'll put photos and short notes here as I go: streets, food, conversations, and whatever surprises me along the way. I'll write in English and Mandarin. My Chinese is still a work in progress, which is part of the point.

First stop: Tokyo.
