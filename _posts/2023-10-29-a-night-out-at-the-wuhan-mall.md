It was around 8:30 p.m. on the way to the mall, and the streets were still full of people. By Ottawa standards, it felt late for a crowd like that. Vendors lined the way, including one selling fresh durian at a price I could hardly believe.

{% include responsive-image.html src="/images/going-to-wuhan-mall1-2023-10-29.jpeg" alt="Crowded Wuhan street on the way to the mall at night" article=true %}

{% include responsive-image.html src="/images/wuhan-mall-fresh-durian1-2023-10-29.jpeg" alt="Fresh durian for sale at a busy street stall in Wuhan" article=true %}

The mall made an impression before we even went inside. Its curved exterior and bright storefronts offered a glimpse of the fancy side of Wuhan. Then we walked in and I was stunned by how polished the place looked, with its sweeping levels and lights rising overhead.

{% include responsive-image.html src="/images/wuhan-mall-outside1-2023-10-29.jpeg" alt="Curved exterior of the Wuhan mall at night" article=true %}

{% include responsive-image.html src="/images/wuhan-mall-inside1-2023-10-29.jpeg" alt="Looking up through the bright, layered interior of the Wuhan mall" article=true %}

And, of course, I gravitated toward the tech stores, including Huawei, and the cars on display. Seeing these vehicles inside the mall felt like a glimpse of future technology. I kept moving from one display to the next, looking at the designs and interiors.
