The lineup outside the breakfast shop was long. I took that as a sign that the food would be good.

{% include responsive-image.html src="/images/wuhan-breakfast1-2023-10-26.jpeg" alt="People lining up outside a breakfast shop in Wuhan" article=true %}

I was right. The steamed buns were some of the best I've had: full of juices, with dough thin enough to make the meat filling feel generous, but sturdy enough to hold everything together. Getting through the lineup was worth it.

{% include responsive-image.html src="/images/wuhan-breakfast3-2023-10-26.jpeg" alt="Juicy steamed buns with a generous meat filling" article=true %}

And then there were hot dry noodles, an absolute favourite of mine. I can get them in Ottawa, but not quite this fresh. Eating them here, in the middle of a Wuhan breakfast crowd, was something else.
