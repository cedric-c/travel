I was getting milk tea from Gong Cha when I discovered that the lady behind the counter unfortunately did not accept Visa. She did, however, accept something known as an Octopus Card, which is, in theory (do you see the foreshadowing yet?), a very practical way to pay for things and use public transit in Hong Kong.

Here’s the thing: you need to load it with Hong Kong dollars (more foreshadowing).

Fine. I’ll just use Apple Pay and finally make use of my bank’s 0% foreign exchange fees.

Nope.

You can’t select HKD in the Octopus app. Instead, you have to select a foreign currency, giving Octopus the opportunity to slip one of its eight tentacles into your wallet and help itself to a 6% fee.

Six percent. 六 (liù). SIX.

I won’t be had by some eight-legged, wallet-frisking cephalopod.

So I marched over to the nearest ATM, where I graciously allowed a different financial institution to frisk me for a fee of less than 6%.

I paid cash.

It’s about the principle.
