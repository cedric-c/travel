I started my day a little later than usual, having gone to bed at 2 AM. Eventually, I made my way to the M+ Museum in the scorching 27°C heat. This is what's known as Canadian melting weather.

Along the way, look at these bad boys. They look exactly like Apple's old trashcan Mac Pros. Hopefully these stay in service longer than the Macs did.

![Trash bins resembling Apple's old cylindrical Mac Pro]({{ '/images/hkd1_trashcan1-2026-10-09.jpeg' | relative_url }})

My watch went rogue and told me to get off the bus a little too late. I ended up with a longer walk to the museum, but I would've missed these views otherwise.

![View on the walk to M+ Museum]({{ '/images/hkd1_museumWalk1-2026-10-09.jpeg' | relative_url }})

![Another view along the way to M+]({{ '/images/hkd1_museumWalk2-2026-10-09.jpeg' | relative_url }})

![Approaching M+ Museum on foot]({{ '/images/hkd1_museumWalk3-2026-10-09.jpeg' | relative_url }})

I spent most of the morning and early afternoon inside M+. There's enough to say about it that it got [its own post]({% post_url 2026-10-09-m-plus-museum %}).

After the museum, I came across a skating rink inside a mall. I'm starting to think you can judge a top-tier city by how many skating rinks it has inside its malls. I saw the same thing in Wuhan.

![Indoor skating rink in a Hong Kong mall]({{ '/images/hkd1_skating-2026-10-09.jpeg' | relative_url }})

Later, on my way to find a snack, I found Bruce Lee instead.

![Bruce Lee statue beside Victoria Harbour]({{ '/images/hkd1_bruce1-2026-10-09.jpeg' | relative_url }})

From there, I walked along the path hugging Victoria Harbour.

![View along the Victoria Harbour waterfront]({{ '/images/hkd1_walk1-2026-10-09.jpeg' | relative_url }})

By evening, this was the view across the water.

![Sunset over Victoria Harbour]({{ '/images/hkd_evening2-2026-10-09.jpeg' | relative_url }})

What I like most about the shots with people in them is that they remind me of those children's books I used to read in the library. The pages were filled with objects, some deliberately hidden, and you had to find them. Almost like *Where's Waldo?*, but for objects.

Here, it's people. Everyone is doing something different: texting friends or family, looking out towards the horizon, laughing at each other's jokes. I like looking around the photo and noticing what each person is up to.

![People walking and sitting along Victoria Harbour at sunset]({{ '/images/hkd1_evening2-2026-10-09.jpeg' | relative_url }})

And finally, the money shot.

![Red-sailed boat against Hong Kong's illuminated skyline]({{ '/images/hkd1_evening-2026-10-09.jpeg' | relative_url }})
